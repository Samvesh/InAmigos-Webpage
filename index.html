About Us

We Believe Every Life Deserves Dignity

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-led nonprofit organization committed to driving meaningful social change. We work at the grassroots level — bridging gaps in education, mental health awareness, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment.

From rural schools to urban communities, our volunteers bring warmth, knowledge, and resources to those who need it most. We believe that small acts of kindness, multiplied across thousands of hearts, can reshape the world.

Where Your Support Makes a Difference

Three flagship initiatives driving real-world transformation across communities.

Education

Project Shiksha

Providing free tutoring, learning kits, and digital literacy workshops to underprivileged students in rural and semi-urban areas. Shiksha has transformed over 2,000 young lives so far.

2,000+ students reached across 14 districts

Environment

Green Amigos

A citywide tree-planting and waste-awareness campaign that has seen thousands of saplings planted and plastic-free zones established through community-driven action.

10,000+ trees planted in 3 years

Mental Health

MindMatters

Breaking stigma and building emotional resilience through peer-support circles, counselling drives, and mental wellness workshops targeting youth aged 15–25.
